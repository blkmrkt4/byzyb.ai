The law

Nothing ever moves.

Content does not migrate when a product ships. The byzyb card is permanent — every product I've ever started keeps its card there forever, including the dead ones. Launching only adds a page on pklabs.ca and changes two fields.

The don'ts

Seven things that break it.

Every one of these is easy to do by accident and annoying to undo.

Never publish the same words at two URLs.

It doubles the maintenance and splits the search signal between the two domains. Link instead.

Never move a page from one site to the other.

That's what the law above exists to prevent. Moving breaks links and forces the decision again every launch.

Never delete a product card from byzyb.

Retired, abandoned and never-funded are all part of the record. Change the status, keep the card.

Never put a personal essay on pklabs.ca.

It dilutes the company site, and it entangles my writing with an asset I might one day sell or take a partner into.

Never put terms, privacy or refunds on byzyb.ai.

Those commitments have to be issued by the registered business, not by me personally.

Never list an unshipped product on pklabs.ca.

A company site full of things you can't buy reads as vapour. The build enforces this — only products with a pklabs block appear.

Never hand-edit a product blurb inside a site's HTML.

The product grids are generated. Edit shared/products.json and run the build, or the next build overwrites you.

Recipes

The four things I'll actually do.

Change a product's copy

  1. Edit its blurb in shared/products.json.
  2. Run node shared/build.mjs.
  3. Both sites now say the same thing. Commit and push.

Launch a product

  1. In shared/products.json, set status to "Live".
  2. Add a pklabs block: slug, tagline, platform, url.
  3. Run the build. It writes pklabs/products/<slug>.html and relinks the byzyb card.

Publish an essay

  1. New HTML file in byzyb/, styled like the others.
  2. Add a card to the Perspective section of byzyb/index.html.
  3. If it's relevant to a product, add one link from pklabs.ca — not a copy.

Record a release

  1. Add an entry at the top of pklabs/changelog.html.
  2. Product and version as the heading, the date, two or three lines.
  3. Say what changed for the person using it. No "bug fixes and improvements".
