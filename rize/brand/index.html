01 · POSITION

Brand Architecture & Thesis

One Brand. Three Formulations.

RIZE is the single consumer-facing brand. Three formulations — RIZE Athletic, RIZE Calm, and RIZE Alert — share a single identity system, a single physical form, and a single brand voice.

The electric yellow slash through the Z is the primary visual identifier across all touchpoints — packaging, digital, social, retail.

Delta Reconciliation v1.0 framed a parent-brand / sub-brand architecture with LOM as the consumer line. That structure has been retired. RIZE is the single public brand. LOM exists internally only and is no longer referenced in any consumer-facing material.

Brand Thesis

"For the moment before the hard part."

This is the primary brand hook. It is broad enough to span every audience — the powerlifter and the parent at school pickup — while specific enough to name what RIZE actually does.

"A breath tool. A flip cap. A botanical hit that you inhale the moment before you need to bring it."

This is the site-wide thesis line. It compresses category, mechanism, and experience into a single sentence.

Delta Reconciliation The v1.0 mission ("To make the focused inhale a universal athletic ritual — as natural as chalk, as deliberate as the belt") has been retired entirely — along with the related "Chalk is a ritual. The mouthguard is a ritual…" Athletic line. Both were too lifting-specific to survive the audience expansion beyond serious gym-goers. Don't revive them. The Athletic pull quote is now "Sharpened breath. Ready for the effort."

Brand Personality

RIZE IS

Focused · Minimal · Confident · Athletic · Premium · Raw

NOT

Intense · Plain · Arrogant · Exclusive · Expensive · Aggressive

Internal-Only Pillars

The Earned / Functional / Precise / Universal framework remains as an internal brand reference but is no longer used as a public callout block. The Breathe / Focus / Move triplet stays only as the top strap line on packaging — it is not a public brand statement.

Delta Reconciliation Both pillar systems were public-facing in v1.0. They have been moved to internal use only. The "rule of three" pattern has been retired as a stylistic device — trios appear only where genuinely natural.

04 · POSITION

Audience & Use Cases

Major Reframe v1.0 positioned powerlifting / CrossFit / serious-gym as the beachhead audience. That framing has been retired. Powerlifters are now considered "the smallest audience." The real audience is dramatically broader — multi-sport athletes, everyday-carry consumers, and the moments before any kind of pressure or transition.

RIZE is for anyone who needs to shift state quickly without putting anything in their stomach or bloodstream. The product earns its place by being used with the person, not just by them — pulled out at the moment, then back in the pocket.

12 · VOICE

Vocabulary — Use & Avoid

Use

breath tool botanical hit botanical lift flip cap flip inhale the moment before pocket-sized stimulant-free invigorating forest in your pocket bring it lock in ritual focus deliberate earned snap hit

Avoid — Regulatory

These create regulatory exposure (Health Canada NHP, UK/EU medical device classification). The noun "inhaler" carries asthma / medical / poppers associations — the verb "inhale" is fine. Quantified physiological claims of any kind read as fabrication and create exposure:

inhaler pocket inhaler 2-second onset 90% effective works in two breaths 20 minutes to kick in
The Inhaler Ban "Inhaler" as a noun is removed from all public-facing copy. The verb "inhale" remains acceptable. "Pocket inhaler" is replaced everywhere with "pocket breath tool."

Avoid — Wellness / Spa

These pull the brand toward aromatherapy and spa territory, which is off-brand. "Meditative" is banned outright — including on Calm:

aroma aromatherapy meditative

Avoid — Hype

revolutionary elite game-changing next level unlock science-backed biohack stack superfood pre-workout energise maximize hustle grind beast mode crush it gains

Avoid — Tube / Object Language

"Tube" reads clinical / toothpaste-y. "Pocket calm" collides with the RIZE Calm SKU name:

tube pocket calm

Outside the Current Vocabulary

These phrases were explored during brand development and sit outside the current vocabulary set. They aren't part of the brand voice today — don't reintroduce them as alternatives.

pocket diffuser pocket vessel scent stick two-breath ritual a black case botanical compass scent-shift

Audience Expansion v1.0 photography direction was gym-centric (Product Hero / Athlete in Action / Instructor or Class). v2.0 expands this significantly. The audience is multi-sport plus everyday-carry. Imagery must reflect that breadth.

Approved Contexts

Product Hero

Dark studio. Single key light, 30–45° offset. Product only — no hands, no context. Used for packaging shots, hero banners, and PDP main images.

Athlete In Action

Broadly defined. Hockey bench, baseball dugout, tennis change-over, basketball sideline, mountain bike climb, gym floor, parkrun start line. Sweat, breath, focus — the moment between effort. Not just lifting.

Everyday Carry

School pickup line, car commute, kitchen at 6pm, train, plane, handbag pull-out. The product as daily ritual, not just pre-workout. This context did not exist in v1.0.

Visual Language

Shoot

Dark backgrounds. Product and subject emerge from shadow. Dramatic single-source directional lighting. Neutral to cool colour grading, slight desaturation. 35mm or 85mm lens equivalents. Raw, editorial feel.

Never Shoot

Bright white backgrounds. Stock commercial fitness aesthetic. Obvious product placement poses. Competing brand logos. Warm orange-filtered gym imagery (except Barry's specifically). AI-generated imagery with incorrect product scale.

APPENDIX A · REGULATORY

Health Canada — NHP Path

This appendix covers the regulatory steps required to legally sell the Athletic SKU (menthol/camphor formula) in Canada. High-level only — the goal is to understand the shape of the process, not to pre-commit to any specific timeline or spend.

Why This Is Needed

In Canada, products administered by inhalation are not classified as cosmetics. A product containing menthol and camphor intended to be inhaled requires a Natural Product Number (NPN) from Health Canada before it can be legally sold. Pre-market approval — you cannot sell first and apply later.

Calm and Alert may or may not require the same path depending on their final formulations.

Step 1 — Determine Application Class

Health Canada groups NHP applications into three classes based on how well the product matches existing pre-approved monographs. Menthol and camphor inhalation products have an existing monograph — which likely puts the Athletic SKU in Class I or Class II.

Class I
Formula matches a single monograph exactly. Review: ~30–60 days.
Class II
Spans multiple monographs, or minor deviations. Review: ~90 days.
Class III
No supporting monograph. Review: 180–210+ days. Unlikely to apply here.

Step 2 — Prepare the Application

Product Licence (PLA)

Submitted via Health Canada online portal. Requires full ingredient list with INCI names, concentrations, dosage form, route of administration, recommended use, and risk info.

Site Licence

Any Canadian site that manufactures, labels, packages, or imports the product needs a site licence confirming GMP compliance.

Labelling

NHP labels must be bilingual (English / French). NPN must appear on the label once issued. Label drafts reviewed as part of the application.

Who To Talk To

Regulatory Consultants — Toronto

Dicentra · dicentra.com — NHP and cosmetic regulatory specialists.

Quality Smart Solutions · qualitysmartsolutions.com — broad Health Canada compliance practice.

Both offer initial consultations. Worth a call to each before committing.

Health Canada Directly

Health Canada's NNHPD accepts general inquiries and can confirm product classification before a formal submission. Free and can save significant time.

Rough Timeline

Classification
Days to a week — consultant or direct call
Application prep
A few weeks once formula is finalised
Review (Class I/II)
30–90 days from submission
Total
A few months from start to NPN in hand

Application fees are currently zero for NHP product licences. The most useful first step is a single call to confirm whether the Athletic formula qualifies as Class I or II.
