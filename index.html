A portfolio of products spanning AI, security, and the tools I wished existed.

SaaS · Live

Ezsay

Visit→

AI writing has a tell - a flatness you can spot even when you can't name it. That tell is costing people grades, credibility, and deals. Ezsay finds it before your reader does: it scans your draft across six dimensions - detectability, AI artifacts, quality, plagiarism, citations, tone - and flags each one with a plain-language reason, so you learn to stop writing like a chatbot instead of just erasing the evidence. It also verifies your citations and converts between eight academic styles, from APA to Bluebook.
iOS App · Live

Language Match

Flashcards teach you words. They don't teach you the nerve to use them on a stranger. Language Match pairs you with real conversation partners through swipe-based matching, then coaches you mid-chat - swipe right on any message for an instant translation, left for grammar fixes and better phrasing. It adapts to your level, tracks vocabulary, and speaks it back to you.

Desktop App · Live

Liquid Relay

Film editors bouncing a project between machines. Developers compiling on one box and testing on another. Both need files moving instantly - and both keep hitting the firewall or security policy that blocks the obvious route. Liquid Relay moves files, clipboard content, and screenshots directly between Mac and Windows machines - no cables, no cloud upload, no NAS, nothing for the network to catch.

SaaS · Experiment

Project ARC

Ask a multinational who owns a given compliance obligation, and the honest answer is a shrug - it's buried in someone's spreadsheet, or nobody's. Project ARC centralizes every legal, privacy, and security requirement across every jurisdiction you operate in and routes each one to its owner. One map - obligations, owners, status - instead of a chase across inboxes and trackers. Built on Next.js, TypeScript, and Prisma.

SaaS · Near launch

WellKept

What was prescribed, what's in your hand, and how you're actually taking it - most trackers blur all three into one number. WellKept keeps them distinct: snap a photo of a vial, patch box, or pill bottle to log the regimen in one tap, see a deterministic view of where you sit in your dosing curve alongside plain-language interaction warnings, and track only the signals you care about - sleep, mood, energy, side effects. Refill math tells you the exact date you'll run out. Built multi-party from day one for caregivers and a read-only doctor view; a wellness tool, never medical advice.

SaaS · In development

Numara

Your net worth lives in a dozen logins and a spreadsheet you update twice a year, if that. Numara photographs the statement - bank, brokerage, mortgage, crypto - and extracts balances and holdings into one continuously updated picture of what you own and owe, with no manual spreadsheet upkeep. Sensitive values are encrypted with a key held separate from the database by design, and every model call runs through OpenRouter so you stay model-agnostic. Installable as a PWA for a private, phone-first view of your whole financial position.

SaaS · In development

HippoOS

Everything you've read, written, or been sent already contains the answer - you just can't find it, and it can't find you. HippoOS compiles emails, documents, and files into a portable, backlinked markdown wiki that an agent navigates - the files are the ground truth, the database just a rebuildable index over them. Every statement carries provenance back to its source with the cited span highlighted, an owner-authored correction overlay lets you fix what a source got wrong, and a built-in MCP server passes that grounded context to any agent. Reachable from the web, your desktop, or your phone.

SaaS · Internal tool

Soul System

Most builds start with no design system at all - which just means the agent decides, page by page, what a button looks like this time. Soul System replaces that improvisation with a 13-step wizard - vibe, color, modes, rules, components - that outputs a structured prompt Claude Desktop builds straight from: CLAUDE.md, DESIGN.md, tokens, per-component specs, a light/dark mood board. One decision made upfront, instead of sloppy code, inconsistent buttons and pages, and hundreds of hours of rework later. Save components once to a reusable library, and each future system takes 10-15 minutes instead of a day.

SaaS · Internal tool

Photo Seeder

When you're building an application, profile-driven apps need real-looking people in real-looking moments - and most builders have one stiff headshot and a stock photo library to work with. Photo Seeder turns one reference photo into 36 identity-consistent lifestyle shots of the same person - wine bar, hiking trail, gym, beach, dinner party, café morning - using any image model on OpenRouter. Drop in a face, pick a model, and walk away: results stream live into the browser and land directly in your filesystem. Open source, model-agnostic, local-first.

Physical Product · Concept

RIZE

Coffee needs twenty minutes and a kettle. Some moments only give you twenty seconds. RIZE is a pocket-sized inhalation device built for that gap: two breaths deliver a rapid sensory and physiological shift - no caffeine, no stimulants, no digestion lag. Three formulations target different moments: athletic performance, calm transitions, and sharp focus. Effects clear within minutes without elevating heart rate or creating dependency. Not a supplement, not a drink - a state-shift tool for athletes, professionals, and anyone navigating high-pressure transitions.
