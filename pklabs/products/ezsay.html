Ezsay

Find the AI tell before your reader does.

AI writing has a tell - a flatness you can spot even when you can't name it. That tell is costing people grades, credibility, and deals. Ezsay finds it before your reader does: it scans your draft across six dimensions - detectability, AI artifacts, quality, plagiarism, citations, tone - and flags each one with a plain-language reason, so you learn to stop writing like a chatbot instead of just erasing the evidence. It also verifies your citations and converts between eight academic styles, from APA to Bluebook.
