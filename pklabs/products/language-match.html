Language Match

Practise on real people, with a coach inside the chat.

Flashcards teach you words. They don't teach you the nerve to use them on a stranger, which is where most people stall after a year of apps. Language Match pairs you with real people to talk to, matched the way a dating app does it, then puts a coach inside the conversation. Swipe right on any message for an instant translation, left for grammar fixes and better phrasing, so you can keep the conversation going instead of leaving it to look something up. It tracks the vocabulary you actually use, adapts to your level, and reads messages back to you so you hear the words before you have to say them.
