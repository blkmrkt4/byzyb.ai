**Purpose of this file:** Single-source reference for Claude Code working on the RIZE website or related materials. Contains the brand position, vocabulary rules, product details for all three SKUs, color system, and copywriting conventions.

**Authority:** This file reflects RIZE Brand Master v2.1 and the current production website. When this file and earlier docs disagree, this file is correct.

---

## 1. What RIZE Is

RIZE is a pocket-sized **breath tool** — a small, deliberate object you flip open and inhale to shift your state. It's engineered for the moment before something important: a heavy lift, a hard call, the school pickup line at 3pm, the start line of a race.

It is **not**:
- A supplement, nootropic, or wellness shot
- An energy drink or stimulant product
- A medical device or therapeutic aid
- An "aromatherapy" or spa-wellness product
- An asthma inhaler (the word "inhaler" is **banned** — see Section 4)

It is:
- A botanical inhalation experience — no stimulants, no caffeine, no crash
- Pocket-sized, lives in a kit bag / handbag / glovebox / back pocket
- Used as often as needed — no tolerance, no taper, no dosing
- A state-shift device that activates the moment you flip the cap

### The thesis line (verbatim)

> "A breath tool. A flip cap. A botanical hit that you inhale the moment before you need to bring it."

### The secondary tagline (verbatim)

> "One flip from a botanical hit."

## 3. The Lexicon (Vocabulary System)

Three primary phrases rotate across all RIZE copy. **They are not assigned 1:1 to specific SKUs** — they deploy based on what each piece of copy needs to convey.

| Phrase | What it does | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| **Breath tool** | Names the *category*. Preferred descriptor for headers, page titles, hero labels. | Functional, athletic |
| **Botanical hit** | Names the *experience*. Pairs well with Athletic and high-energy contexts. | Punchy, immediate |
| **Botanical lift** | Softer alternative to "botanical hit." Pairs well with Alert/Calm where "hit" would feel too punchy. | Premium, refined |

### Two-word SKU descriptors

Each SKU has a two-word descriptor that captures its mood in a short, repeatable form. These are a vocabulary pool — deploy where they serve the copy, not as forced labels on every piece.

- **Sharpened Breath** (Athletic) — used selectively, rarely alone
- **Grounded Breath** (Calm) — usable in isolation
- **Bright Moments** (Alert) — carries the dual personality

## 4. Banned Vocabulary

These words/phrases must **never** appear in RIZE copy.

### Regulatory exposure
- **"Inhaler"** as a noun — carries asthma/medical/poppers associations. The verb "inhale" is fine.
- **"Pocket inhaler"** — same reason
- **Quantified physiological claims** of any kind — e.g., "2-second onset," "90% effective," "works in two breaths," "20 minutes to kick in." These create regulatory exposure and read as marketing fabrication.

### Wellness / spa register
- **"Aroma"** — sounds spa, undermines athletic positioning
- **"Aromatherapy"** — same reason
- **"Meditative"** — banned even on Calm

### Hype vocabulary
- "Revolutionary," "elite," "game-changing," "next level"
- "Unlock," "science-backed," "biohack," "stack"
- "Superfood," "pre-workout," "energise"
- "Maximize," "hustle," "grind," "beast mode," "crush it," "gains"

### Tube / object language
- **"Tube"** — too clinical / toothpaste-y
- "Pocket calm" (as a phrase) — collides with RIZE Calm SKU name

### Outside the current vocabulary
Phrases that were explored during brand development but aren't part of the brand voice today. Don't invent these as alternatives:

- pocket diffuser
- pocket vessel
- scent stick
- two-breath ritual
- a black case
- botanical compass
- scent-shift

### Other rejected reference quotes
- **"Chalk is a ritual. The mouthguard is a ritual…"** — this was an early Athletic pull quote. Now retired. Don't revive — it leans too gym-specific for the broad audience.

## 5. Brand Voice

### Core principles
1. **Earned, not marketed.** RIZE doesn't shout. It states.
2. **Functional, not emotional.** Lead with what the product does. Emotion follows from truth, not from describing the emotion directly.
3. **Short sentences.** Compressed. If a sentence can be shorter without losing meaning, cut it.
4. **Speak like an athlete to a training partner.** If the line sounds like ad copy, rewrite it.

### Brand voice examples (verbatim)

**Do:**
- "Flip. Inhale. Back in the room."
- "On for a breath. Off when you're done with it."
- "The one you bring with you."
- "You'll know if it works the first time you flip the cap."

**Don't:**
- "Experience the power of nature in your pocket."
- "Take RIZE everywhere you go for peak performance!"
- "Fast-acting formula provides immediate energy boost."
- "Clinically proven to enhance focus and alertness."

### Approved vocabulary (the words RIZE uses)

ritual · focus · perform · inhale · flip · deliberate · earned · recover · snap · hit · before · pull · working set · platform · floor · block · pocket · lock in · stimulant-free · invigorating · forest in your pocket · bring it · the moment before · breath tool · botanical hit · botanical lift

## 8. Color System

### Core palette

| Variable | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0A0A0A` | Primary background (near-black, ~65% of all visual weight) |
| `--bg-2` | `#0F0F0F` | Secondary background, card backgrounds |
| `--ink` | `#F0F0F0` | Primary text on dark |
| `--mute` | `#7A7A7A` | Secondary/label text |
| `--line` | `#1F1F1F` | Borders, dividers |
| `--gold` | `#F4EE35` | **Electric Yellow** — primary brand accent. Used in: slash through Z, headline highlights, marquee dividers, footer accent line. **Replaces the previous warm gold (#C4952A) as of v2.1.** |
| `--premium-gold` | `#C4952A` | **Reserve color** — used only for premium / limited / collaboration releases. Not in standard production. |
| `--coral` | `#FF5F3A` | Athletic SKU accent |
| `--sage` | `#6BBF8A` | Calm SKU accent |
| `--ice` | `#3AAFFF` | Alert SKU accent |

### Color rules
1. **Black dominates** — every composition starts with near-black. ~65% visual weight.
2. **Yellow is the mark, not the fill** — Electric Yellow is reserved for the slash through Z and primary accents. Never as a background fill or large block.
3. **SKU accents are SKU-locked** — Coral / Sage / Ice apply only to their SKU. Never used interchangeably.
4. **Gold is reserve only** — Premium gold appears only on premium / limited / collaboration releases. Never standard production.

### CSS class naming note
The site has a CSS class named `.gold` that resolves to `var(--gold)` (now Electric Yellow). The class name is legacy from when the brand color was warm gold. Behavior is correct; renaming is purely cosmetic.

## 9. Typography

### Web stack (currently used on the live site)
- **Display / wordmark:** Anton (Google Fonts) — used as web-safe sibling for Impact
- **Body:** Inter Tight
- **Accent / labels / stats:** JetBrains Mono

### Print / packaging stack
- **Display / wordmark:** Impact
- **Headers:** Arial Black
- **Body:** Arial

### Typography rules
- Wordmark "RIZE" is always uppercase, never "Rize" or "rize"
- Character spacing on the wordmark: 8pt minimum, 18pt maximum
- Don't stretch, skew, condense, or rotate the wordmark
- The Z has a skewed slash through it in Electric Yellow — this is part of the wordmark, not optional

## 10. The Hero Carousel (Site Behavior)

The hero on the site displays a single static dark bottle. The variant name (ATHLETIC / CALM / ALERT) and accent color **dissolve into specks** and reassemble as the next variant. The bottle body never moves between variants — only the colored elements change.

### Timing
- **2.8s** visible (pristine text, no specks)
- **0.4s** dissolve out (mask scatters the text)
- **0.35s** pure black (variant fully invisible)
- **0.35s** clean fade-in to next variant (no mask — the new text appears cleanly)

The text MUST appear pristine (no mask/specks) for the full visible interval. The mask is *only* applied during the dissolve-out phase.

## 11. Packaging Form Factors

### Form Factor A — Flip-Cap (Primary)
- Rectangular, Zippo-style proportions (~42mm × 18mm × 92mm)
- Matte black HDPE/PP plastic body
- Flip-top with audible snap, silicone gasket seal
- Matte / velvet soft-touch surface
- 35g declared on packaging
- 60–90 uses per unit
- **Cap matches body — same dark finish. No coloured caps in standard production.**

### Form Factor B — Screw-Top (Alternative, Under Evaluation)
- Rectangular body with squared-off corners
- Rectangular matte cap, two-turn screw close with click-to-seat
- Mesh diffuser sits flush at rim — no nozzle, no nostril contact
- **Cap matches body — same dark finish as flip-cap.**

### Label architecture (both form factors)
- **Top strap:** BREATHE · FOCUS · MOVE · Variant number (01 / 02 / 03)
- **Wordmark:** RIZE in Impact (print) / Anton (web), with Electric Yellow skewed slash through the Z
- **Variant name:** ATHLETIC / CALM / ALERT in variant accent color
- **Accent slash:** Skewed bar in variant accent color
- **Bottom strap:** Front-face descriptor + STIMULANT-FREE · 35g

## 14. How to Use This File

If you (Claude Code) are building or modifying RIZE materials:

1. **For voice/copy decisions**, treat Section 5 (Brand Voice) and Section 4 (Banned Vocabulary) as binding.
2. **For color/CSS work**, use the CSS variable names in Section 8 — they're consistent with the production codebase.
3. **For SKU copy**, the verbatim hook lines and pull quotes in Section 2 are the canonical wording. Don't paraphrase unless explicitly asked.
4. **For new copywriting**, the Lexicon (Section 3) is your vocabulary pool. Pull from it; don't introduce new category words without asking.
5. **For anything regulatory or claim-related** (timing, efficacy, physiology), default to silence unless the user explicitly provides a defensible number. The banned list in Section 4 is non-negotiable.
6. **When in doubt about a phrasing**, ask. Do not invent brand-level language.
