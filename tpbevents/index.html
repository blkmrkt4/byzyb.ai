Why padel

The fastest-growing sport in the world is built for teams

Padel is doing to corporate networking what golf did for a previous generation — except everyone gets to play, and nobody loses a morning.

The court is small, the ball is slow off the glass, and the game is doubles by design. A first-timer and a lifelong athlete can share a court and both walk off laughing. That's not a marketing line — it's why the sport grew to 50 million players worldwide, and why luxury clubs and resorts are converting tennis courts into padel courts as fast as they can pour concrete.

"Tennis is difficult if you are a beginner… but everyone can have fun from the very first moment."
— Diego Zalazar, GM, PPL champions NY Atlantics

It's social, it's fast, it's gender-inclusive, and it works for any age or fitness level. Which makes it the rare offsite activity that doesn't quietly exclude half the room.

The format

Your team. Polar Bears pros. A multi-court experience.

This isn't a clinic where a coach shouts from the sideline. Our Toronto Polar Bears Pro Padel League players are on court, in the rallies, setting your CFO up for the smash.

A

Learn it in ten

A fast, funny intro from the Polar Bears pros gets everyone rallying inside ten minutes. The glass walls do the forgiving — the ball comes back.

B

Play with a Polar Bears pro

Rotating doubles with a Toronto Polar Bears professional on court. Mixed pairings shuffle every round, so the exec team actually mixes instead of clustering by department.

C

Compete for something

A short round-robin tournament with a final the whole group watches. Trophies, trash talk, and a Polar Bears exhibition rally to close.

Run of show

Ninety minutes or a half day — same spine

The run of show is flexible — we shape it around your group — but here's an example. The full version below fills a half day. Compressed, it plays as a tight 90-minute session: intro, pro rotations, exhibition, done. Morning or afternoon, weekday or weekend.

0:00

Arrival & espresso

Coffee bar open, rackets fitted, kit handed out. Polar Bears pros circulate and meet the group before anyone touches a court.

0:20

The ten-minute game

Polar Bears pros run a rapid, high-energy intro. Rules, grip, the glass. Everyone is rallying before they've finished their coffee.

0:45

Pro-partner rotations

Rotating doubles across multiple courts with the Polar Bears pros. Pairings reshuffle every round — designed so every person gets court time with a pro.

1:45

The Polar Bears Cup

Short-format round robin into a final. The rest of the group watches courtside with drinks. Winners take the cup; everyone takes the photo.

2:30

Exhibition rally

The Polar Bears pros play a full-speed exhibition set — the moment everyone films. This is what padel looks like at league level.

2:50

Food, awards & the debrief

Catered lounge session. Awards, team photos with the Polar Bears pros, and the unstructured hour where the real conversations happen.
