The platform in practice

From signals to orchestrated action

Three kinds of signal feed one model of each relationship. The platform turns it into a specific next step, and learns from the response.

1 · Signals

Market signalsResearch, a simulated population of clients, and live interviews read for tone, pauses and hesitation.
Relationship signalsContact rhythm, who initiates, response times and the sentiment of correspondence.
Client recordsThe history already held in the systems a business runs today.

2 · Intelligence

Relationship dynamicsWho needs attention, and whether the team is keeping up with each client.
Client intelligenceWhat matters to each client, built up over time.
Message calibrationWhat is likely to land with this client, in what tone, and how often to be in touch.

3 · Orchestration

Opportunity orchestration
  • Who to contact
  • When
  • What to say, and how
  • Through which channel
The team reviews each recommendation before it is sent.

4 · Feedback

Direct and simulated feedback
  • Did the client respond?
  • With what sentiment?
  • Did the relationship strengthen?
  • How did reality compare with the simulation?
↺Every response and its sentiment refine the models.
↺Real responses recalibrate the simulated population.

Evidence stays distinct

ObservedContact history, correspondence, interview responses and records held in existing systems.
InferredWhere a relationship stands, and what is likely to help.
TestedWhether the client responds, with what sentiment, and whether the relationship strengthens.
SimulatedA population of simulated clients used to compare messages before sending, and recalibrated against real responses. A simulated response is never treated as a client's.

Where the platform began

Our experience spans more than thirty years of client relationships, leading large businesses, and building and using AI systems. During work on a large enterprise CRM deployment, we saw the gap between what the records held about clients and how those relationships were actually changing. We built a listening engine to close that gap. Our platforms carry that work forward.
